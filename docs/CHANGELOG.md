### 11:28 · model(hindcast): run predicted vs observed shift hindcast
- What changed: ran src/hindcast.py (task 4), which trains each species' model on 2009–2015 tows only (BOTTEMP + AVGDEPTH, lat/lon/year asserted out), builds 0.2° early (2009–2015) and late (2019+) grids kept to the 555 cells sampled in both periods, and writes data/processed/hindcast.csv. This is the honest diagnostic, not a bug: the hindcast is a credibility check on the forward projections, shown as-is in the demo.

### 11:34 · model(grid): build 0.2deg grid and warming-scenario suitability

- How to check it: `python3 src/scenarios.py` prints 2409 tows from 2016+ → 635 cells → 530 kept → 9540 prediction rows (530 cells × 6 species × 3 scenarios) and mean suitability per species/scenario. grid_predictions.csv has 9540 data rows, 12 columns, no NaN, suitability in [0,1]. Warming direction is sensible: longfin squid 0.743→0.838, summer flounder 0.263→0.296 and scup 0.215→0.241 rise +0→+2°C, while the cold-water contrast Atlantic cod 0.149→0.075 and silver hake 0.739→0.634 fall.

### 11:59 · feat(overlap): compute area-weighted lease suitability signals
- What changed: added src/overlap.py (task 6.1). load_leases() reads the BOEM Offshore_Wind_Leases_outlines layer and dissolves it by lease number to 45 active leases (one geometry each). lease_signals() builds each 0.2° grid cell as a square polygon, reprojects leases and cells to EPSG:5070 (equal-area), intersects them, and takes the area-weighted mean suitability per (lease, species, scenario). Percent change = (scenario − +0°C baseline)/baseline. Signal thresholds and the four suggestion-text strings are locked-in constants quoted verbatim from design.md: Rising ≥ +10%, Falling ≤ −10%, Stable in between; Uncertain first if species AUC < 0.70, or < 20 tows from 2016+ within 25 km of the lease, or the lease overlaps no cells; species with < 50 presences are skipped. join_revenue() is a documented stub left for task 6.3.
- How to check it: `conda run -n shifting-grounds python src/overlap.py` prints 45 active leases, 540 signal rows (45 × 6 species × 2 warmed scenarios), 36 leases overlapping ≥1 grid cell, the signal counts (Stable 167, Uncertain 132, Rising 129, Falling 112) and example signals. Hand-check: Revolution Wind (OCS-A 0486) black sea bass +0→+2°C 0.470→0.683 (+45.3%, Rising), recomputed independently over its 3 overlapping cells — matches.
- Known issues: none. Uncertain (132 rows) comes entirely from the coverage rules, not AUC: 108 from 9 leases outside the survey footprint (Gulf of Maine, Carolinas, Pacific) that overlap no cells, and 24 from 2 overlapping leases with < 20 nearby 2016+ tows. All 6 species have ≥ 604 presences, so none is skipped. lease_signals.csv is written later by task 6.3 (revenue join); 6.1 computes the signals only.
